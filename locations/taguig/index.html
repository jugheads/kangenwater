Is a Kangen Water Machine Right for Your Needs?

Kangen Water machines combine filtration with electrolysis to produce water with different characteristics, depending on the model and settings. They should not be treated as interchangeable with every type of water purifier.

Filtration performance depends on the specific filter, while electrolysis changes water chemistry, including pH. A higher pH does not, by itself, demonstrate that water is safer or that contaminants have been removed. Do not assume an ionizing machine can make untreated or microbiologically unsafe water suitable for drinking.

Before choosing a machine, ask TrueKangen for the relevant product manual, source-water requirements and documented filtration capabilities. If your main concern is a particular contaminant, identify that concern first and check whether the proposed system is designed to address it.

For Residential Use

Think about how your household currently gets drinking water and where a machine would fit into your routine. Useful questions include:

Is the incoming water supply suitable for the proposed model? Is there enough counter space near a compatible water connection? How much drinking water does your household use each day? Who will handle filter changes and routine cleaning?

For a condominium or rented home, check any building or landlord restrictions before planning changes to plumbing or fixtures.

For Office Use

Office buyers should consider shared use, not just the number of employees. Peak demand, pantry space, ease of operation and responsibility for maintenance all affect whether a machine is practical.

Ask about the intended operating conditions of the model you are considering. Confirm whether it suits your expected usage and how staff should identify the correct settings for drinking water. Any non-drinking-water outputs should be clearly distinguished and used only as directed by the manufacturer.

What to Check Before You Buy

A useful product comparison goes beyond the initial machine price. Request a written breakdown of what is included and what may involve additional costs.

Installation and Water Compatibility

Share details about your faucet, available space and water source. Photos may help begin the discussion, but they do not replace any necessary compatibility checks.

Confirm connection requirements, whether adapters or other components are needed, and who will perform installation. Installation availability, charges and arrangements should be confirmed directly rather than assumed from TrueKangen’s Taguig address.

Filters, Cleaning and Ongoing Costs

Ask for the recommended filter replacement schedule and cleaning procedure for the exact model. Maintenance needs can vary with water conditions and usage.

Also check replacement filter availability, consumable costs and any servicing requirements. Understanding these commitments helps you compare the full cost of ownership rather than purchase price alone.

Warranty and Product Documentation

Before paying, request the current warranty terms, including who provides warranty support, what is covered and what exclusions apply. Check the model name and included accessories against your quotation.

For claims about contaminant reduction or product certification, ask for documentation that applies to the specific model and filter—not just general statements about the brand.
