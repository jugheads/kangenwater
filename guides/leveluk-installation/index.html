Leveluk Installation: A Practical Guide to Getting Set Up

A successful **Leveluk installation** starts with checking your exact machine model, water supply, faucet connection and electrical setup. Do not assume every Enagic Kangen Water Machine uses the same fittings or installation procedure.

TrueKangen distributes Enagic Kangen Water Machines and provides personalized guidance for residential, office and personal use. Based at RCBC Center in Bonifacio Global City, Taguig, the team led by Bernard Huela offers free assistance with machine selection and questions about getting started.

This guide explains what to check before installation and when to seek help. Your model’s current official manual should remain the primary reference for assembly, operation and safety instructions.

1. Identify Your Model Before Buying Accessories

Find the full model name on the machine, packaging or purchase documentation. Then locate the matching official installation instructions.

Before unpacking fittings or purchasing adapters, confirm:

The intended installation arrangement for your model. Which hoses, connectors and accessories are included. Whether your faucet or plumbing requires an additional approved fitting. The machine’s electrical rating and water-supply requirements.

A fitting that works with one faucet or machine may not work with another. Avoid forcing connections or substituting parts without confirming compatibility.

If you are unsure what you have, send TrueKangen the model name and clear photographs of the machine’s connection points and your sink setup.

2. Check Your Water Supply

Do not assume that a water ionizer makes any source water safe to drink. Filtration and ionization are different processes, and a machine’s treatment capabilities depend on its specifications and filter.

Confirm that your incoming water meets the requirements in the model manual. If your supply comes from a private well, storage tank or another source with uncertain quality, arrange appropriate water testing and professional advice before relying on it for drinking.

Ask about any known sediment, hardness or other water-quality concerns. Additional treatment may be appropriate, but it should address a verified issue rather than an assumption. Do not choose a pre-filter solely because it is advertised as compatible with water ionizers.

3. Assess the Faucet and Installation Space

For a model intended to connect at a faucet, the faucet’s outlet design matters. Pull-out spray heads, unusual aerators and non-standard threads may need a different connection approach; compatibility should be checked before installation.

Photograph the faucet from the side and underneath the outlet. Include any visible brand or model information. These details can help identify whether an approved adapter is available.

Choose a stable location that allows:

Access to controls and routine maintenance points. Hose routing without kinks, stretching or accidental pulling. Placement away from direct splashes and excessive heat. Outlet and discharge arrangements that follow the manual.

Do not improvise hose extensions, outlet heights or drain connections. These details can affect operation and should follow model-specific instructions.

4. Confirm Electrical and Plumbing Requirements

Check the voltage and other electrical requirements on the machine’s rating label. Do not assume that a plug adapter makes a unit electrically suitable for your location.

Keep plugs, sockets and connections dry. If the available outlet is damaged, poorly positioned or of uncertain suitability, have a qualified electrician assess it before use.

For a connection involving plumbing modifications, ask a qualified plumber to review the proposed arrangement. Confirm with TrueKangen or the manufacturer whether the planned installation is supported and whether any requirements affect warranty coverage.

TrueKangen’s supplied business information does not establish on-site installation availability, coverage areas or installation fees. Request confirmation before scheduling work or assuming that free assistance includes physical installation.

5. Complete Setup and First-Use Checks

Follow the official manual’s sequence for connecting hoses, fitting the filter and preparing the machine for first use. Flushing duration, flow adjustment and initial operating steps should come from that manual—not generic online instructions.

During the first run:

Inspect accessible connections for leaks. Confirm that water follows the intended outlet paths. Check the display for warnings or error messages. Follow the instructions for selecting water intended for drinking.

Do not treat every output setting as drinking water. Use each setting only for the purpose specified by the manufacturer.

If there is leaking, an unusual smell, an electrical concern or a persistent error, stop using the machine and seek guidance. Avoid opening the housing or attempting internal repairs.

Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I install a Leveluk machine myself?**

That depends on the model and connection arrangement. Follow the manual and seek help if faucet compatibility, electrical suitability or plumbing changes are uncertain.

**Will it fit my existing kitchen faucet?**

Not necessarily. Share faucet photographs and model information before ordering an adapter. Appearance alone does not establish compatibility.

**How long does Leveluk installation take?**

A reliable estimate requires checking the machine, faucet, fittings and any required plumbing work. Ask for an estimate after those details are reviewed.

**Does TrueKangen provide free installation?**

TrueKangen offers free assistance. Whether hands-on installation is available, and whether it carries a charge, needs separate confirmation.
